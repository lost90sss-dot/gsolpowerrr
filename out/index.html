Solar Energy

Harnessing the Power of the Sun

Solar Energy Extraction Process

01

Photon Capture

Photovoltaic cells absorb solar photons, generating electron-hole pairs in the semiconductor material through the photoelectric effect.

02

Electron Excitation

Electrons gain energy and move through the cell's internal electric field, creating a directional flow of electrical current.

03

DC Conversion

Direct current flows through external circuits and inverters, converting DC to AC power for grid integration and storage.

04

Grid Distribution

Clean renewable energy is distributed to buildings or fed back to the grid, with smart metering tracking production in real-time.

Wind Energy

Capturing the Power of the Wind

Wind Energy Extraction Process

01

Wind Intake

Wind passes through turbine blades, creating rotational force. Optimal wind speeds (12-35 mph) generate maximum kinetic energy.

02

Blade Rotation

Aerodynamic blade design converts wind pressure into rotational motion, spinning the rotor shaft at increasing speeds.

03

Generator Conversion

Mechanical rotation drives a generator, converting kinetic energy into electrical energy through electromagnetic induction.

04

Power Distribution

Generated electricity flows through power converters and inverters, supplying clean renewable power to grid or on-site systems.

Hydro Energy

Harnessing the Power of Water

Hydro Energy Extraction Process

01

Water Collection

Water is collected in reservoirs behind dams, creating gravitational potential energy from elevation differences.

02

Pressure Buildup

Water accumulates behind the dam, building hydrostatic pressure as depth increases, storing massive amounts of potential energy.

03

Turbine Spin

Controlled water flow spins turbine blades at high speed, converting kinetic energy into mechanical rotational energy.

04

Power Output

Connected generators convert turbine rotation into electricity, providing consistent, controllable renewable power to the grid.

Geothermal Energy

Harnessing Heat from the Earth

Geothermal Energy Extraction Process

01

Heat Source Access

Deep wells reach geothermal reservoirs where rock temperatures exceed 300°C, tapping into Earth's constant internal heat.

02

Fluid Extraction

Hot geothermal fluids or steam are pumped to the surface through production wells, carrying thermal energy from deep underground.

03

Energy Conversion

High-pressure steam or hot fluid drives turbines, converting thermal energy into mechanical rotation for electricity generation.

04

Continuous Power

Spent fluids are reinjected to maintain pressure and heat, enabling reliable baseload power 24/7 with minimal environmental impact.
